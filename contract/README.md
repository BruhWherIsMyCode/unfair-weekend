## Published 0.3 (13 Nov 2026)

What is now APPLIED, in the contract and in the code on the phone path (the iPhone parent app streams pictures and sound to the team backend; the backend runs Agent 3's call session):

1. **One-hand phone script.** The person holds the phone in one hand. The script lines `setup_phone`, `task_arms` and `repeat_arms` changed text and there is a new line `swap_hands`; the texts are in `contract/script.en.json` (owner-written) and are the only source. The arms task is one 15 s capture (v11; was 12 s): she holds the phone in her right hand and raises her left hand beside her face while `task_arms` plays ("...now..."), hold 1-6.5 s; the call plays `swap_hands` 6.5 s into the arms capture (a `cues` entry of the task definition, `at_ms` 6500), then she swaps and raises her right hand, hold 8.5-15 s. Each hold gives her up to 3 s to raise the hand before a missing hand counts (`ARMS_RAISE_GRACE_MS`) and is measured from the first frame her hand was seen. The backend's `checkin_backend/data/script.en.json` is a byte-identical copy (a backend test pins it); the four changed lines must be prepared again (`prepare`), unchanged lines are reused.
2. **`video_path` on `kind: say`.** A `command.execute` with `kind: say` carries an optional `video_path` exactly as `backend/VOICE_VIDEO.md` "iOS playback handoff" defines it (issue #13): present only when the exact audio of that line has a prepared MP4, fetched with the demo header, played with AVPlayer instead of the PCM track, `playback.completed` only after real playback end. Without `video_path` the client plays the PCM audio as before.
3. **`concern_raised` and `concern`.** The summary status `concern_raised` and the optional summary field `concern` `{"raised_by": "agent", "reason", "note"}` (reason one of `said_unwell`, `asked_for_help`, `confused_answers`, `reading_far_outside`, `other`; note at most 200 characters) are applied: the call agent raises it with `alert_family`, the backend stores one family message for the call with kind `concern_raised`, a plain-words reason and the line "If she seems unwell, call 112." Status order: `change_noticed` > `concern_raised` > `incomplete` > `learning` > `as_usual`. A later `change_noticed` replaces a `concern_raised` message for the same call; a `change_noticed` is never replaced by anything calmer. Weakness or numbness said in the call is `said_unwell`.
4. **Required tasks on the phone path are smile and arms.** The phone sends raw audio and nothing measures the sentence on the server yet, so the sentence task is not part of a phone call (no sentence module is registered there, the call driver skips it) and cannot make a call `incomplete`. The `sentence` script lines stay in the script for the PC version and the speech build.
5. **Backend entry.** The team backend calls one function for an answered call: `analysis.backend_entry.run_backend_call(link, modules=None, *, env=None)`. It builds the `CallSession` over the backend's `ClientLink`, loads her usual (baselines), runs the call driver, stores the baseline values the call may add, writes the call record JSON and returns the SessionResult dict. Environment variables (all optional): `CALL_DRIVER` (`fixed` or `claude`; unset follows `UW_CLAUDE=on`), `UW_CLAUDE_BACKEND` (`api` default, or `subscription`), `UW_SAFETY_FLOOR` (only `off` turns the floor off), `UW_MIRRORED` (`on`/`off`, default off), `UW_BASELINE_PERSON` (baseline key, default the person id), `UW_DATA_DIR` (baseline folder), `UW_BASELINE_NEEDED` (whole number >= 1: normal calls until her usual is ready, default 5), `UW_RUNS_DIR` (call record folder), `UW_CALL_LOG` (`off` hides the live log lines). An invalid value is a `ValueError` before the call starts.
6. **Baseline hygiene.** A call adds nothing to her usual when it is labelled `acted`, when a change was confirmed, when the status is `concern_raised`, or when ANY usable final reading in the call was outside her usual (confirmed or not).

**Issue #10 (where is the measuring done).** For the weekend the phone sends pictures (JPEG, 640x480, every 200 ms) and the server measures them with Agent 3's stream tasks. Measuring on the client exists as the PC client protocol (`analysis/pc/protocol.md`: the client measures every frame and sends `reading`, the server runs the same task modules on the readings) and is the later iPhone path; nothing in the phone boundary has to change for it except the sample `format`.

## Earlier deltas (status)

These were proposed on 13 Nov 2026 before the draft was published. Status after 0.3:

1. **Arms task.** APPLIED (0.3). The owner decided the person holds the phone in one hand, so the arms task is one free hand at a time held beside the face (the existing `analysis/hand_metric.py`). The lines `setup_phone`, `task_arms` and `repeat_arms` were changed (texts in `script.en.json`).
2. **Sentence module is not implemented in this version.** APPLIED as a stub on the phone path; on the PC version it measures (delta 13). On the phone it is not registered at all, see 0.3 item 4.
3. **Arms task, one hand at a time (v6).** APPLIED. One `arms` task (the Task enum is unchanged), one capture window, two halves. v11 timing: 15 s (duration 15000, the contract maximum; 200 ms JPEG samples): 0-1 s settle, 1-6.5 s her LEFT hand held beside her face, 6.5-8.5 s swap, 8.5-15 s her RIGHT hand (v6 was 12 s: 1-5 / 5-7 / 7-8 settle / 8-12). The script changes are applied in the owner's wording (`swap_hands` is played when the swap starts; the other three lines are in `script.en.json`). `fixtures.json` keeps the historical 12000 of draft 0.2.
4. **Task definition field `cues`.** APPLIED. A task definition may carry `"cues": [{"at_ms": 5000, "line_id": "swap_hands"}]`: lines the call plays at that offset after capture started, without blocking sample intake. Arms has one; smile and sentence have none.
5. **Adapter `listen(question_id, timeout_s)`.** APPLIED (the backend's `ClientLink.listen`). Returns the transcript of her answer after a question line played, or nothing when nothing was heard within the timeout (8 s).
6. **Arms baseline is per hand.** APPLIED. The arms task is compared against a baseline for each hand: `hand_left` and `hand_right` (reading `hand_sink`, stored as `arms.hand_left.hand_sink` and `arms.hand_right.hand_sink`). The Reading reports the hand that is further outside, with `side` that hand. Two outside readings confirm only on the same direction and the same side.
7. **PC link protocol v1 (v7).** APPLIED on the PC version only (`analysis/pc/protocol.md`). Client to server: `hello`, `start_call`, `status`, `capture_started`, `reading`, `capture_ended`, `cancel_ack`, `said`, `answer`, `hang_up`. Server to client: `hello_ok`, `say`, `listen`, `start_capture`, `cancel_capture`, `progress`, `call_result`, `error`. The phone does not use it (see issue #10 above).
8. **SessionResult status `concern_raised` and field `concern` (v7).** APPLIED on the phone path in 0.3 (item 3). Family text: "<name> said or showed something during today's check-in that needs a look (<reason in plain words>). Please call her. If she seems unwell, call 112."
9. **Numbers to the call agent (v7).** APPLIED. The call agent sees today's reading from `run_task` (`value`, `usual_low`, `usual_high`, `outside_usual`, `distance`, `side`, `unit`) and `baseline_ready`. Confirmation and the family message stay in code.
10. **Required tasks are smile and arms (v7).** APPLIED, and it is the rule on the phone path (0.3 item 4).
11. **Link protocol v2: speech (v8).** APPLIED on the PC version only (`analysis/pc/protocol.md`: `stt_token_request`, `speech_result`, `stt_token`, `say` with `audio_pcm16k_b64`). On the phone the sentence is not measured yet.
12. **Single-use token flow (v8).** APPLIED on the PC version only. The ElevenLabs API key exists only in the server's environment; the client asks for a single-use `batch_scribe` token and sends back the transcript and word timings, never audio.
13. **Sentence metric `speech_duration_v1` (v8).** APPLIED on the PC version only. Primary reading: how long she takes to say "Today I am sitting by the window.", metric id `speech_duration_v1`, unit `s`, sign `speech`, `side` always null, baseline key `sentence.duration_s`. Outside her usual means LONGER than her usual high only. Quality is `unusable` with `speech_to_text_failed` or `nothing_heard`.
